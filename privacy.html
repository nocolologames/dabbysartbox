Privacy Policy

Dabby's Art Box · Effective October 22, 2026

Dabby's Art Box is a coloring and drawing app for young children. It is private by design: it collects no personal information and sends no data to us or to anyone else.

No data collection

There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and the app contains no third-party code. It has no chat, no social features and no way for a child to contact anyone.

What stays on your device

Drawings, finished pictures, stickers, progress and settings are stored on your device. A grown-up can add up to four kids; each kid has a name you type in and a color. The name is optional, stays on the device, and is only used to label that kid's art in the app.

Optional iCloud backup

iCloud backup is off unless a grown-up turns it on in the app's grown-up area. When it's on, the kids, their drawings and their progress are copied to your family's own private iCloud account so they can come back on a new or reset device. That copy belongs to you, stays inside your Apple account, and is never visible to us. Turning backup off stops new copies; deleting a kid in the app removes that kid's art from the backup too.

Saving, printing and sharing

A grown-up can save a picture to Photos, print it, or share it, only after passing a grown-up gate. Saving to Photos only adds pictures; the app never reads your photo library. Anything shared goes only where the grown-up chooses, through iOS's own share sheet.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase that opens every page, brush and sticker is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store payment details or who bought it. Purchases sit behind the grown-up gate and can be managed in your App Store account settings.

Children

The app is made for kids and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Everything a child makes stays on the family's device and, if a grown-up turns it on, in the family's own iCloud.

Deleting data

Deleting a kid in the app's grown-up area deletes that kid's drawings and progress. Deleting the app deletes everything on the device. If iCloud backup was on, the backup can also be removed in iOS Settings under your Apple account, iCloud, Manage Storage.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
