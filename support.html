Dabby's Art Box Support

Coloring and drawing for kids, with a giggly paint blob named Dabby.

Common questions

How do I get to the grown-up area?
On the island map, tap the small gear in the bottom left corner, then type the number shown in words (for example FORTY-SEVEN: type 4 then 7). After three wrong answers the gate rests for a minute.

What's free, and what does the purchase open?
The first 2 pages of every island, 6 brushes and 25 stickers are free. One purchase opens all 70 pages (each with an easy and a detailed version), all 12 brushes and every sticker. There's no subscription, and Family Sharing is on, so one purchase covers your whole family.

I bought it, but the padlocks are still there.
In the grown-up area, tap Restore purchase. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account (or one in the same Family Sharing group) that made the purchase.

How do time limits work?
In the grown-up area, pick a daily limit. Two minutes before the end, Dabby yawns; at the limit the drawing is saved and Dabby takes a nap until tomorrow. A grown-up can add 15 minutes or turn the limit off for the day from the nap screen.

We got a new iPad. Where are the drawings?
If iCloud backup was on in the grown-up area, sign in to the same iCloud account on the new device, open the app, and turn iCloud backup on again; the kids and their drawings come back.
